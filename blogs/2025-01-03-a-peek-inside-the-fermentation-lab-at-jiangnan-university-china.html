A Peek Inside the Fermentation Lab at Jiangnan University, China

2025-01-03

This morning, I had the chance to visit the fermentation laboratory at Jiangnan University, and it was honestly such a cool experience. Walking in, I was immediately attracted by the sheer number of machines and the complex setup they have for studying different brewing techniques. It was spectacular to see it all in person and get a behind-the-scenes look at how things actually work in their fermentation lab. What stood out to me the most was the intense level of precision involved. I've always thought of brewing as more of a traditional craft, but seeing their highly precise tools somehow changed my perspective. For the first time, I understood how exact these brewing experiments have to be and how complex each experiment is. It takes a high amount of testing and scientific control to consistently produce good wine and achieve big technological breakthroughs. Seeing how advanced the technology has become was interesting, and it gave me a clearer understanding of the science of fermentation. When I shared researchers with the results of my own homemade rice wine project (which I had been dealing with a frustrating issue where my rice wine was growing mold with little black dots), they gave me some guidance, explaining that the room where I fermented it probably just wasn't clean or sterile enough. The happiest moment was when I heard that despite the surface mold, the rice wine underneath actually fermented really well! Getting that professional feedback really gave me a huge boost of confidence. I walked away with clear next steps to fix my environment, and I'm so excited to make my next batch even better!
