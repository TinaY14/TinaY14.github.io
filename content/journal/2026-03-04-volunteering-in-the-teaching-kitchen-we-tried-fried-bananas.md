This week in the kitchen, we made fried bananas, which was a totally new experience for me. I usually just eat bananas raw for my daily fruit intake, or maybe mash them up into pancake batter, but I had never actually tried frying them before. We paired them with a really unique, fresh mix of beans, pineapple, red onion, and cucumbers.
Honestly, the dish wasn't bad, but it’s probably just a one-time thing for me. Watching the process, I couldn't help but notice just how much oil went into the pan. I'm usually very cautious around fried foods since they aren't the healthiest option, but I have to admit that they did turn a really beautiful golden color while cooking. For my taste, the heavy frying process actually ended up masking the natural sweetness of the banana. Still, I'm glad that I tried something new with bananas - but that side dish made from pineapple and bean mix was very delicious. I loved the flavor of it so much, I'd be more than happy to just make a bowl of that on its own!
